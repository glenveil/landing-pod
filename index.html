Process

four acts

I

Listen to the room

We map the human ritual before naming the automation.

II

Draw the invisible line

We define what the agent may do, may ask, must cite, and must refuse.

III

Craft the behavior

Prompts, tools, memory, retrieval, and interface details are tuned like joinery used in violin making.

IV

Age it in use

We test with real work, then remove noise until the system feels inevitable.

Founded between craft and computation

We make agents that behave like apprentices from a patient century.

The studio imagines artificial intelligence not as spectacle, but as a specialist craft. Each system is commissioned, named, trained, tested, and refined until it can carry work across the threshold with calm precision.

The result is a gentle juxtaposition: Japanese quietness inside a French workshop, paper ledgers beside model context, hand-drawn lines beside reliable automation.

Manifesto

Less dashboard. More companion. Less feature pile. More single-purpose mastery. Less artificial personality. More graceful conduct.
